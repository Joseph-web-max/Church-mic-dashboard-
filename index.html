📖 How to use this dashboard

A. Using the laptop speakers (no amplifier)

  1. Click 💻 Laptop Speakers. The volume is limited to 60% for safety.
  2. Click 🎤 Turn Mic ON and choose Allow when the browser asks for the microphone.
  3. In Microphone, choose your microphone. In Speakers, check it says Speakers (Realtek(R) Audio).
  4. Press 🔊 Test speakers. If you hear the tone, the output works.
  5. Speak and watch the Microphone level bar move. Keep it mostly in the green.
  6. Click 📡 Go ON AIR to send your voice to the speakers.

B. Using the church amplifier

  1. Plug the laptop’s headphone jack into the amplifier’s AUX / LINE IN with an audio cable. Turn the amplifier volume down first.
  2. Click 🔌 Amplifier, then 🎤 Turn Mic ON.
  3. In Speakers, choose the headphone or line-out output, then press Test speakers and raise the amplifier volume slowly.
  4. Click 📡 Go ON AIR and set the loudness with the Speaker volume slider.

During the service

  • EMERGENCY MUTE (or press M or Esc) silences the speakers instantly. Press it again to unmute.
  • 📡 Go OFF AIR stops sending your voice. 🎤 Turn Mic OFF releases the microphone completely.
  • ⏺ Record Sermon starts recording. Press it again to stop, then click the download link that appears.
  • Keep the page open and the laptop plugged in. Do not close the browser tab while preaching.

If something is wrong

  • Level bar moves but no sound: check the pill at the top says ON AIR, not MUTED. Press Test speakers, then check the Speakers list and the volume slider.
  • Level bar does not move: choose another microphone in the list, and check the microphone is on, charged and paired in Windows.
  • Screeching (feedback): lower the volume, move the microphone away from the speakers, or press Emergency Mute. The Feedback guard also lowers the volume by itself.
  • Voice sounds thin or delayed: this is common with Bluetooth microphones. A wired or USB microphone sounds better.
  • Microphone blocked: click the padlock next to the page address, allow the microphone, and reload the page.
Before every service: arrive early, do a short test with someone listening at the back, and use Chrome or Edge.
Using the laptop alone (no amplifier)? Sound plays from the laptop speakers, or from headphones if plugged in. Use the Test speakers button to check the sound, and wear headphones while testing, because a microphone close to laptop speakers causes screeching.
Avoid feedback (screeching): keep the microphone away from the speakers and start with the volume low, then raise it slowly. Connect the laptop’s headphone output to the amplifier’s AUX / LINE IN with a cable, and set Windows to use that output.
Bluetooth microphones may sound thinner and add a small delay. For best quality use a wired or USB microphone, or the microphone’s own USB receiver.
